Who We Are

A private company with a long view.

Cotten-Bratton Energy Company is a privately held minerals and royalties company. We acquire mineral rights and royalty interests and manage them for the long term, not as line items in a portfolio but as lasting stakes in the land and the people who work it.

We keep our footprint small and our standards high. Every interest we hold is researched, verified, and managed with the same care we'd expect if the roles were reversed.

What We Do

Focused work across minerals, royalties, and leases.

01

Mineral & Royalty Acquisition

We purchase mineral and royalty interests directly from owners. Every offer is built on current title, production, and market data, so the number holds up against your own.

02

Lease Brokerage

We broker oil and gas leases between owners and operators, handling valuation, terms, and bonus through to a signed lease.

03

Title & Ownership Research

We trace title and confirm what an interest actually covers, so owners and buyers know the ground before a deal moves.
